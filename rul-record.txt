V ta namen sem pripravila anketo, v kateri so vinogradniki delili svoja zaznavanja. Odgovore sem analizirala in jih predstavila besedilno ter z grafi. Poleg anketnih odgovorov v diplomski nalogi uporabim tudi ustrezno literaturo o Beli krajini, s pomočjo katere predstavim naravnogeografske značilnosti preučevanega območja, ki pomembno vplivajo na vinogradništvo. Ugotavljam, da ima Bela krajina ugodne naravne predispozicije za vinogradništvo s posebnim tipom zmerno celinskega podnebja. Posebnost oz. značilnost so mesečna in tudi letna padavinska odstopanja od dolgoletnega povprečja. Vinogradniki so v anketi odgovarjali, da tako ali drugače zaznavajo spremembe v podnebju. Omenjajo predvsem spremembe v razporejenosti padavin skozi leto in naraščanje temperatur.. Te spremembe pa so privedle tudi do bolj pogoste pojavnosti bolezni vinske trte, sprememb v kakovosti prsti, zamik fenoloških faz, kakovosti grozdja in ekonomske spremembe pri pridelovanju in prodaji vina.\n\n\nJezik:\tSlovenski jezik\nKljučne besede:\tklimatogeografija, lokalno podnebje, podnebne spremembe, vinogradništvo, Bela krajina\nVrsta gradiva:\tDiplomsko delo/naloga\nOrganizacija:\tFF - Filozofska fakulteta\n\nLeto izida:\t2024\nPID:\t20.500.12556/RUL-158650 \nDatum objave v RUL:\t19.06.2024\nŠtevilo ogledov:\t926\nŠtevilo prenosov:\t227\nMetapodatki:\t  \n\nCitiraj gradivo\nNavadno besedilo\nBibTeX\nEndNote XML\nEndNote/Refer\nRIS\nABNT\nACM Ref\nAMA\nAPA\nChicago 17th Author-Date\nHarvard\nIEEE\nISO 690\nMLA\nVancouver\n:\t\nKopiraj citat\nObjavi na:\t\n\n\nSekundarni jezik\nJezik:\tAngleški jezik\nNaslov:\tThe perception of climate change among winegrowers in the White Carniola region\nIzvleček:\n\nThe purpose of the thesis is to determine to what extent and how winegrowers in White Carniola perceive climate change in their vineyards. For this purpose, I prepared a survey in which winegrowers shared their perceptions. I analysed the responses and presented them in text and with graphs. In addition to the survey responses, the thesis also incorporates relevant literature on White Carniola, through which I present the natural geographical characteristics of the study area, which significantly influence viticulture. I find that White Carniola has favourable natural predispositions for viticulture with a specific type of temperate continental climate. The peculiarity or characteristic is monthly and annual precipitation deviations from the long-term average. Grape growers in the survey responded that they in one way or another perceive changes in the climate. They mention changes in the distribution of rainfall over the year, in temperatures, and in the climate. These changes have also led to more frequent occurrences of vine diseases, changes in soil quality, delays in phenological phases, grape quality, and economic changes in wine production and sales.\n\n\nKljučne besede:\tclimatogeography, local climate, climate change, vine-growing, White Carniola\n\n\nPodobna dela\nPodobna dela v RUL:\t\nPodobna dela v drugih slovenskih zbirkah:\t\n\nNazaj\n\nOperacijo delno financira Evropska unija iz Evropskega sklada za regionalni razvoj ter Ministrstvo za izobraževanje, znanost in šport.
